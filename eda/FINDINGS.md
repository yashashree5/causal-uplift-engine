## Summary: what to change in cleaning

One line per decision; the table under *Findings* has the evidence and caveat for each (`#n`). These are proposals for the cleaning owner, not agreed rules.

1. **Campaign date is unknown; the leakage check is `blocked`.** (#1) Give every time-based feature a cutoff parameter; recency uses the provisional anchor `2019-03-18 23:40:03`.
2. **Do not use `first_redeem_date`; treat `first_issue_date` as noisy.** (#2, #3) 11.2% of clients redeem after the last purchase; 11.8% are issued after their first purchase.
3. **Set ages outside [16, 100] to missing and add an `age_invalid` flag.** (#4) 3,190 clients (0.80%); keep them.
4. **Aggregate `purchase_sum` and the points columns once per (client_id, transaction_id).** (#6) A row-wise sum overstates spend 10.34x.
5. **Key a transaction by (client_id, transaction_id).** (#7) 28 ids are shared by two clients.
6. **Points spent are stored as non-positive numbers: take the absolute value.** (#10) 585,800 non-zero spent values (regular and express) are negative; received points are never negative.
7. **Keep zero-quantity and zero-sum rows, flag them; null `trn_sum_from_red` = 0 plus an indicator.** (#9, #11) `trn_sum_from_iss` is not a proven line amount (owner decision).
8. **Read every ID as a string.** (#13) 6.2% of client ids start with '0' and 2,967 look like scientific notation.
9. **Keep gender `U`; encode missing product attributes as an explicit 'unknown' level.** (#5, #12)
10. **Labels need no cleaning: use `uplift_train` only.** (#14) Treated share 49.98%, uplift +3.32 pp; by segment, age_band: +1.34 pp (016-024) to +5.90 pp (065-100). (#15)
11. **`uplift_test` has no labels; the tables join cleanly. Keep that as assertions in the job.** (#17, #18)
12. **History is short (117 days; first and last months partial): use daily or weekly windows.** (#19, #16)

Open decisions that block final features: the campaign date, and whether `items.amount` comes from `trn_sum_from_iss` (see *Open questions*).

## Open questions (not answerable from the data)

1. What is the real campaign (SMS) date? Until it is set in `campaign.cutoff`, the leakage check stays blocked.
2. Is `trn_sum_from_iss` the right source for the contract field `items.amount`? Decision needed from the contract owner; the data only show approximate agreement with `purchase_sum`.
3. Why do 28 transaction ids belong to two clients, and why are card issue dates after first purchases?
4. What do `U` (gender), zero `product_quantity` and null `trn_sum_from_red` mean in the source system?
